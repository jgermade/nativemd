# Silent argument limits in the harness

## 2026-11-25

- Found by the Sonic 1 client: `nmd-oracle` kept at most 16 `--capture` options and
  dropped the rest without a word; a capture group of 26 routines left 10 of them without
  any captured game state for several check runs (their suites ran on synthetic states
  only, and passed).
- Fixed: up to 64 captures, more is an error. The same "silently drop" pattern elsewhere is
  now an error too: more than 4096 states in a suite, more than 4 `--sym` (suite, oracle,
  hybrid). `nmd-statefuzz` already rejected its limits and bad ranges.
- Lesson: a harness that silently ignores input hides missing checks; limits must fail
  loudly.
